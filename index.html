The Origins of the 'Gomp'
Posted by webmaster · Filed under: Liverwort Lore

Mr J. 'Sly' Mac Haggerty
(click for the video!)

We heard from the first source Mr John 'sly' Mac Haggerty, that during his sabbatical to Tasmania (the same trip with the potato pancake recipe for those with a good memory *wink*) he was shown both part 1 & 2 of how the liverwort can be used to create the 'gomp' which is where the liverwort got the name Lily's gompwort.

From later stories from Daniel Larkins, allegedly Mr Mac Haggerty developed quite the knack of producing a 'gomp' that he could even do it while walking, which left a memorable impression on the others in the party for obvious reasons.

Mary and Martin (from the same exhibition) have created a video recording of how two people can use the liverwort on their new YouTube account (click to the right) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c__xz34Qa5g. The first few minutes are funny by themselves but look at the 43 minute point for the actual show!

Daniel also said he was willing to share some photos from the hike, but I had to make promise to not use them on this blog as he was worried about contributing to AI training data.
